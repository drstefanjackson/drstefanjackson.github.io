Future Directions

Research Vision

✦
01
Stimuli-Responsive & Combination Delivery Systems

Building on doctoral work in injectable thermo-responsive hydrogels and PLGA nanoparticles to design multi-drug depot systems with sustained, programmable release at the site of pathology. Sol/gel transition tuning, cross-link density modulation, and rheological optimisation of HA-SH systems form the foundation for biomaterial platforms in chronic pain, neurology, ophthalmic and intraocular biologics delivery, and beyond.

02
Nucleic Acid & Targeted Conjugate Therapeutics

Extending PEG-lipid pDMAEMA polyplex chemistry toward non-viral gene therapies that silence pain-associated ion channels (NaV1.2, NaV1.7) without systemic side effects, while contributing analytical and formulation expertise to the next wave of antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) and lipid-nanoparticle platforms in oncology and rare disease.

03
Lyophilisation, Stability & Process Development for Biologics

Bringing hydrogel and nanoparticle formulation expertise into freeze-dried product development. Hands-on understanding of excipient and bulking-agent selection, lyo- and cryoprotectant systems, and pH and buffer optimisation, paired with thermal characterisation by DSC, MDSC, and freeze-drying microscopy (FDM) for collapse temperature, eutectic point, and Tg′ determination. Cycle development from primary through secondary drying, with attention to cake structure, residual moisture, and reconstitution, in service of lyophilised biologic, mRNA-LNP, and ADC platforms aligned with ICH Q8 to Q12 expectations.

04
Translational Analytical Innovation

Developing and validating LC-MS/MS, HPLC-DAD, multi-attribute, and stability-indicating methods that bridge bench-scale characterisation to regulatory-compliant quality control. Closing the analytical gap between academic drug delivery research and clinical manufacturing readiness in industrial R&D, CDMO, and biotech settings.

What unifies these threads is a patient-centred philosophy: the conviction that the most impactful science begins with the clinical need and works backwards to the molecule, the formulation, and the method.

Profile

Across Industry & Academia

✦

Dr. Stefan Jackson is a Marie Curie Research Fellow (H2020-MSCA-ITN, GA 956477), Novartis BioCamp 2025 Winning Team member, and IVF Budapest 2026 Fully Funded Delegate, holding a PhD in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences and Biotechnology from the University of Camerino, Italy. His thesis, Precision Medicine for Chronic Neuropathic Pain: A Multiplatform Approach to Drug Delivery, was defended on 7 July 2025 and the doctoral title was conferred on 12 December 2025.

With 6+ years of interdisciplinary R&D experience, Stefan specialises in the design, synthesis, characterisation, and analytical method development of advanced drug delivery platforms. His work spans injectable HA-based hydrogels, PLGA/PEG nanoparticles, PEG-lipid conjugates, and pDMAEMA polyplex gene-delivery vectors. He developed and ICH Q2-validated an RP-HPLC-DAD/UV method for crobenetine (BIII 890 CL, Boehringer Ingelheim) and routinely applies LC-MS/MS, HPLC, UHPLC, GC-MS, NMR, DLS, SEM, AFM, DSC, FT-IR, and oscillatory rheometry within a regulatory-compliant framework.

His industrial secondment at Polypure AS (Oslo, Norway) saw him purify monodisperse PEG-lipid conjugates to >98% purity using FPLC and preparative chromatography, monitored by HPLC-MS, working within GMP/ALCOA+ compliance and supporting OOS/CAPA documentation. This experience, combined with active interests in Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADC), Lyophilisation, and biologics formulation, positions him equally well for industrial R&D, postdoctoral research, and research-collaboration roles.

Injectable Thermo-Responsive Hydrogels

Formulated two injectable HA-SH hydrogel systems: (i) Michael-addition crosslinked gels with vinyl-sulfonated PEG-p(HPMAm-lactate) triblock copolymer, and (ii) disulfide crosslinked HA-SH gels.
